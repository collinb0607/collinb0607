## Offline Toolkit

Free desktop apps for jobs that are usually done on websites that cap your file size, add a
watermark, or want a subscription. These run on your own computer: no uploads, no limits, no account.

| Tool | What it does | Get it |
|---|---|---|
| [**GIF Maker**](https://github.com/collinb0607/gif-maker) | Turns a video into a GIF, animated WebP, MP4 or WebM. Hits a target file size and previews the result before saving. | [Windows / Mac download](https://github.com/collinb0607/gif-maker/releases/latest) |
| [**Video Compressor**](https://github.com/collinb0607/video-compressor) | Shrinks a video to fit a size limit, like Discord's 20 MB or an email attachment. Two-pass encoding lands just under the target. | [Windows / Mac download](https://github.com/collinb0607/video-compressor/releases/latest) |
| [**Audio Converter**](https://github.com/collinb0607/audio-converter) | Pulls the sound out of videos, or converts audio, to MP3 or WAV in batches, and can make every file equally loud. | [Windows / Mac download](https://github.com/collinb0607/audio-converter/releases/latest) |
| [**Image Converter**](https://github.com/collinb0607/image-converter) | Converts, resizes and compresses HEIC, WebP, PNG and JPG photos in batches of any size, with drag and drop. Fits each file under a size limit and removes location data. | [Windows / Mac download](https://github.com/collinb0607/image-converter/releases/latest) |
| [**Background Remover**](https://github.com/collinb0607/background-remover) | Cuts the subject out of photos with AI that runs on your own PC: transparent PNG or a colour background, in batches, with a before/after preview. | [Windows / Mac download](https://github.com/collinb0607/background-remover/releases/latest) |
| [**Image Upscaler**](https://github.com/collinb0607/image-upscaler) | Enlarges photos and drawings 2× or 4× with Real-ESRGAN AI on your own graphics chip, adding real detail instead of blur. Batches, with drag and drop. | [Windows / Mac download](https://github.com/collinb0607/image-upscaler/releases/latest) |
| [**PDF Toolkit**](https://github.com/collinb0607/pdf-toolkit) | An offline Acrobat alternative: edit the text and pictures already in a PDF; organize, compress and split; search, comment and mark up; fill in, create and sign forms (including certificate signatures); compare two PDFs. | [Windows / Mac download](https://github.com/collinb0607/pdf-toolkit/releases/latest) |
| [**Subtitle Generator**](https://github.com/collinb0607/subtitle-generator) | Writes subtitles for any video with Whisper AI on your own PC, in 99 languages. Fix them with a live preview, then save an .srt file, a subtitle track, or captions burned into the video. | [Windows / Mac download](https://github.com/collinb0607/subtitle-generator/releases/latest) |
